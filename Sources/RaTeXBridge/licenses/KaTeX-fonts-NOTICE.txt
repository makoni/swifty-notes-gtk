KaTeX fonts — attribution and license notice
============================================

Swifty Notes draws formulas with the KaTeX mathematical fonts (the KaTeX_*
font faces: KaTeX_Main, KaTeX_Math, KaTeX_AMS, KaTeX_Caligraphic,
KaTeX_Fraktur, KaTeX_SansSerif, KaTeX_Script, KaTeX_Typewriter and the
KaTeX_Size1–4 faces). They come unmodified from the KaTeX project
(https://github.com/KaTeX/KaTeX) by way of the RaTeX engine's
ratex-katex-fonts crate, and are compiled into the application binary.
